How to Play

Controls

  • Move: ←/→ or A/D
  • Jump: Space, W or ↑ (tap for a short hop, hold for a long leap)
  • Swim: tap jump to stroke upwards, S/↓ to dive
  • Tail swipe: J or K
  • Mouse: left click tail swipe · right or middle click jump (hold for a long leap, works alongside the keyboard)
  • Pause: P or Esc · Mute: M · Leaderboard: L

Scoring

  • Acorn: +25 · Enemy defeated: +100 · Power-up: +50
  • Time bonus for a fast clear; the Score Multiplier doubles everything for a while

Mechanics

  • Health drains when you are hit; at zero you lose a life and return to the last cairn you touched
  • Water: you float at the surface and can leap out; stay under and your breath runs out
  • Wind: gusts push you sideways, strongest in the air
  • Moving ledges carry you with them
  • The Dullahan is stunned after a charge — that is your moment to strike

Power-ups

  • Speed Boost · Shield · Extra Life · Time Freeze · Score Multiplier · Health Restore
